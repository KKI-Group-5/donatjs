How DonatJS Works

📋

Create a Campaign

Set your goal, deadline, and description. Your campaign goes to admin review before going live.

✅

Get Approved

Admins review your submission. Once approved, your campaign becomes visible to all supporters.

💚

Receive Donations

Supporters donate to your campaign. Reach your target and funds are automatically paid out.

🔒

Safe & Transparent

Missed targets trigger automatic refunds. Fraud detection protects every donor automatically.
